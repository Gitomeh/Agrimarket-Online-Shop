Build a complete, production-quality full-stack web application called:

AGRI MARKET

Tagline:
"Connecting farmers to better markets."

AgriMarket is a modern agricultural marketplace that connects farmers directly with buyers. Farmers can list produce, manage inventory, receive orders, communicate with buyers, and view market intelligence. Buyers can discover produce, compare prices, purchase products, track orders, communicate with farmers, save products, and manage their purchasing activity.

This is a serious Frontend Engineering internship capstone project.

Do NOT build a simple demo, landing page, CRUD prototype, or collection of disconnected screens.

Build a cohesive, polished, responsive, accessible, production-oriented application with realistic workflows, reusable components, strong TypeScript architecture, proper states, validation, error handling, and clean UX.
